The design language

Lock these and every screen — built by you or by Claude Code — stays unmistakably labread.

Palette · ember on void

One accent family — amber through coal — never a second hue. Glass is white at 5–9% opacity, never a flat grey. Color means something: warmer = more vital.

Typography · three voices

Fraunces — the soul
Day 47, and rising.
Emotional headlines, big numbers, the italic accent word. Used with restraint.
Inter — the interface
Steady · Charged · Re-test recommended
Buttons, body, labels. Quiet and legible so the glow can shine.
JetBrains Mono — the instrument
[ FERRITIN ] 38 ng/mL · 74 VITALITY
Eyebrows in brackets, data, units, the console feel.

The signature · biology as light

  • Living glowThe screen is lit from behind the content by warm radial embers that slowly breathe (9s loop). It's the single thing every labread screen shares — and what no clinical app does.
  • GaugesEvery biomarker is a glowing arc, not a bar. A bright bloom marks where you sit. Optimal looks warm and alive; out-of-range never feels like a scolding.
  • RidgesMulti-marker trends are drawn as soft, layered aurora ridges — translucent gradient mountains, exactly like the reference. Data you'd want to screenshot.
  • GlassCards and pills float above the glow with heavy blur and a 1px warm hairline. Generous 20px+ radii everywhere. Nothing is square.
  • AnnaA single ember-gradient avatar. She speaks in Fraunces, short and human — the warm second person in the room, never a chatbot bubble.
