Family rules

separate, not merged

Each profile is its own private record with its own report, protocol, and challenges. Switching profiles swaps the whole app context — you never see two people's blood blended together.

consent is the gate

Adding a member means processing their health data. The flow captures a clear consent step per person — the right foundation for a CNDP-registered relaunch, and a real trust signal.

the cap is a setting

Shown here as you + 2. The limit is one number in config — easy to lift to 3, or tie larger circles to a paid tier later.
