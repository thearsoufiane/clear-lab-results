How the economy works

The rules behind the screens — so points stay meaningful and discounts reward loyalty instead of draining revenue.

The loop · five beats

01
Pick a challenge21, 30, 60, or 90 days — each tied to a real health goal (iron, energy, metabolic, full transformation).
02
Do the daily actions~100 points a day for completing the day. Miss a day, keep the points you earned — the streak just resets.
03
Complete & earnFinishing pays a bonus and unlocks that challenge's offer tier. Longer challenge, bigger reward.
04
Share your storyCompleters are invited to post — the best stories feature on labread.health, feeding trust and growth.
05
Come back tomorrowA new challenge is always queued. The reason to keep using labread is built into the loop.

Offer ladder

  • 21 days20%A first win — easy to reach, proves the loop works.
  • 30 days30%Habit territory. The discount grows with commitment.
  • 60 days50%Serious users. Half off rewards real consistency.
  • 90 days90%The trophy. Gated so it never undercuts the business.
the 90% guardrail

The 90-day, 90%-off month is earned, not given. A member stays subscribed for three months and finishes the full 90-day challenge — then claims one month at 90% off. They've already paid for three; you reward the loyalty with one. Discounts cost you least exactly where engagement is highest.
