how it works

One call in. A report they read comes out.

No dashboard to learn. No portal to migrate. It slots into the workflow you already have.

01 · send

Push the report

PDF, image, or structured data — POST it to labread. We read 50+ biomarkers from Moroccan, French, and international lab formats, in Arabic, French, or English.

02 · process

Math, then language

A deterministic pipeline maps every value to validated clinical ranges — adjusted for age and sex. Only then does a constrained AI write the explanation. It can describe the result. It cannot invent one.

03 · deliver

Your brand, out the door

Structured JSON plus a branded PDF — your logo, your colours, your name. Attach it to the result email, embed it in your portal, or hand it over at the counter.

the integration

A clean API. Nothing else.

You own the presentation layer. We hand back structured biomarker data — names, values, units, ranges, statuses — plus a plain-language explanation ready to render.

why labread

We're not an analytics platform.
We're a translation layer.

Between the numbers you produce and the people who receive them.

Math first, AI second

The pipeline is deterministic — validated clinical ranges, not model guesswork. The AI only writes the sentence after the math has decided the status. It cannot hallucinate a value it never received.

Your brand. Ours invisible.

Your name at the top, your colours, your logo. "Powered by labread" at most — or nothing at all. The patient's relationship stays with you.

Any lab, any language

Moroccan private labs, hospital panels, French formats. Arabic, French, English. We read what your region actually uses — not just what American labs print.

Retention, not just service

A patient who understood their last report comes back for the next one. An explanation isn't a nicety — it's the difference between a transaction and a relationship.

EU-hosted, CNDP registered

Data lives in Paris. Registered with Morocco's CNDP under Law 09-08. Never used to train models. Never sold. You can put that in writing to your patients.
