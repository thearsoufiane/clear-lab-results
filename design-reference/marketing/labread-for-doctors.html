why you can trust the output

The AI never decides the medicine.

This is the part that matters to you, so we'll be precise about it. labread is two layers, and only one of them is a language model.

layer one · deterministic

The math decides

A rules-based pipeline extracts every value and maps it to validated clinical reference ranges, adjusted for age and biological sex. There is no model here. No inference. No hallucination is possible — it is the same arithmetic you would do yourself.

then, and only then
layer two · constrained

Anna explains

A language model turns the result into a sentence a patient understands. She can only describe what the math already established. She cannot invent a number, change a status, diagnose, prescribe, or alarm. She narrates. She never decides.

never diagnoses · never doses · never replaces a clinician
every report ends with: worth discussing with your doctor
