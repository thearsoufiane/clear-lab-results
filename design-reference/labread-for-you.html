Questions

Good to know.

Which labs does labread work with?

Any of them. Upload a photo or PDF from any lab in any country — labread reads the values and standardises the ranges for you, even across different units and languages.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No — and it's not meant to be. labread helps you understand your results and walk into appointments prepared. Always confirm decisions with a licensed clinician.

How private is my data?

Your results are encrypted and never sold. Each family profile is yours alone, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

Can I really start for free?

Yes. Upload your first test and explore your breakdown free. Upgrade only when you want more uploads, profiles, or family tracking.
